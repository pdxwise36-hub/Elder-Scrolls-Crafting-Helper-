Optimal Research Order

Research on one item happens one trait at a time, so your slots let different items run at once. This plan assumes you start now with all slots free — follow the order on the Research Timers tab. Within a single item you can research its listed traits in any order; each research's time depends on its position (#), not the trait.

What Do You Need to Craft?

Add each item you plan to craft — with its style, trait and quality — and see the materials it needs, plus a combined shopping list for everything.

Every crafted item takes 1 style stone, (with a trait) 1 trait stone, and (with an enchant) 1 potency + 1 essence + 1 aspect rune. Improving quality consumes every tier up to your target — so a gold item needs Fine + Superior + Epic + Legendary mats. Amounts default to max Temper Expertise; base materials to 150 (CP160). Adjust all counts above for your level and passives (jewelry base-mat counts differ).

Research Passive (per craft)

Each craft's research passive — Metallurgy, Stitching, Carpentry, Lapidary Research — reduces research time and unlocks more simultaneous research slots. Set the rank you've trained for each.

Other Bonuses

About Champion Points: the live game has no Champion Point star that reduces trait research time — reductions come from the crafting passive and ESO Plus. This "other reduction" field is here so you can fold in any future buff or event bonus yourself. All reductions stack multiplicatively.
