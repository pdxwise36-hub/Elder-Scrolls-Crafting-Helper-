Trait Tracker

Check off every trait you've already researched. Highlighted cells are currently being researched. The count on the right is what drives your next research time for that item.

Research Passive (per craft)

Each craft's research passive — Metallurgy, Stitching, Carpentry, Lapidary Research — reduces research time and unlocks more simultaneous research slots. Set the rank you've trained for each.

Other Bonuses

About Champion Points: the live game has no Champion Point star that reduces trait research time — reductions come from the crafting passive and ESO Plus. This "other reduction" field is here so you can fold in any future buff or event bonus yourself. All reductions stack multiplicatively.
